## 1. Product name

**UK Late Payment & Credit Control Kit** (working name). Alternatives for the founder to choose from: *Paid On Time Kit* · *Credit Control Kit for UK Small Businesses*.

## 2. Product description

A ready-to-use credit control system in Notion for UK small businesses that sell to other businesses. It tracks customers and invoices, tells you the next step for every overdue invoice, works out the statutory interest and fixed compensation you can claim, and includes a full set of chasing templates, a credit control SOP and a payment terms policy.

## 3. Target customer

- **Primary:** UK business-to-business firms with 1–50 staff that invoice on credit terms and have no dedicated credit controller (trades and construction subcontractors, agencies, consultancies, IT and managed services, wholesalers).
- **Secondary:** bookkeepers and virtual assistants who chase invoices for clients.
- **Not for:** consumer-facing businesses, or firms already paying for credit control software integrated with their accounts.

## 4. Problem solved

Late payment ties up cash, and chasing is often ad hoc and awkward. UK law lets businesses claim interest and fixed compensation on late business debts, but working out the right rate, sum and wording takes time. The kit turns this into a weekly 20-minute routine with clear figures. Evidence and sources sit on the linked opportunity record.

## 5. Competitive positioning

- **Free options:** the Small Business Commissioner's calculator and GOV.UK guidance give the rules but not a process.
- **Paid software:** credit control tools such as Paidnice (from about £45–£49 a month) and Chaser (from about £199 a month) automate chasing but cost every month and need an accounting integration.
- **Our position:** one price, no integration, the whole process included and UK rules built into the calculations. The trade-off: reminders aren't sent automatically.

> For UK small businesses that chase their own invoices, the Late Payment Kit is a credit control system in Notion that tells you exactly what to send next and what you can claim. Unlike credit control software, it's a single purchase with nothing to integrate. Unlike free calculators, it runs your whole chasing process.
> 

## 6. Product specifications

- **Platform:** standard Notion databases, relations and formulas. No paid add-ons or integrations.
- **Databases:** Customers, Invoices and Chase Log, linked Customer → Invoices → Chase Log.
- **Calculations:** days overdue; statutory interest (amount × (8% + reference rate) × days ÷ 365); fixed sum by band (£40 / £70 / £100); daily interest; total claimable; reference-rate prompt; next-action ladder.
- **Documents:** Start Here, six templates (0–5) plus a phone script, Credit Control SOP, Payment Terms & Credit Policy Template, How the Numbers Work, QA checklist.
- **Data:** ships only fictional SAMPLE and QA-TEST rows. Buyers' data stays in their own workspace.

## 7. Feature list

**Built in v0.9**

- Customer register with terms, credit limit, risk rating and customer type
- Invoice register with status, chase stage and promised payment date
- Automatic days overdue, interest, fixed sum, daily interest and total claimable
- Rate Check telling the user which Bank of England rate date to look up
- Next Action ladder from pre-due reminder to letter before action, with dispute and payment-plan handling
- Chase Log linked to invoices
- Customer outstanding and overdue totals with a credit check
- Six templates and a phone script, SOP, policy template and numbers guide

**Planned for v1.x**

- Saved views: Overdue, Due this week, Promises due, board by chase stage
- Dashboard page with overdue totals
- Import guide for invoice exports from common accounting software
- Part-payment handling
- Complete-tier extras: customer statement, payment plan agreement and monthly KPI view

## 9. Pricing strategy

- **Core £49:** databases, Templates 0–3, SOP, Start Here.
- **Complete £99:** everything in Core plus the interest claim and letter before action templates, the policy template and v1.x updates.
- **Upsells (hypotheses):** setup session £149; fractional credit control retainer; bundle with the SOP Library System.
- **Rationale:** one payment sits below one or two months of credit control software. These are hypotheses to test with real buyers, not validated prices.

## 10. Delivery model

## 11. Customer onboarding

1. Purchase → delivery message with the duplicate link and the 15-minute Start Here.
2. Add customers and open invoices; delete the sample rows.
3. Tick Claim Statutory Interest where it applies and enter the reference rate.
4. Run the first Monday routine with the templates.
5. Optional paid setup session for buyers who want help.

Follow-up emails are drafted in the commercialisation pack and need approval before anything is sent.

## 1. Product name

**UK Landlord Compliance OS** (working name). Alternative: *Landlord Compliance Calendar*.

## 2. Product description

A Notion system for self-managing landlords that keeps every property's certificates, renewals, tenancies, deposits, repairs and documents in one place, and flags what is due soon or overdue.

## 3. Target customer

- **Primary:** self-managing landlords in England with 1–15 properties who track compliance in spreadsheets or their inbox.
- **Secondary:** small letting agents and portfolio managers who want a simple client-ready tracker (white-label later).
- **Scope:** England first. Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland have different rules and need separate rule packs.

## 4. Problem solved

Landlords juggle certificates on different cycles (gas safety every 12 months, electrical checks every 5 years, EPCs every 10 years) and new duties under the Renters' Rights Act 2025, where some breaches carry civil penalties of up to £40,000. Research found spreadsheets are the norm and some landlords find property software too complex for a small portfolio. Evidence and sources sit on the linked opportunity record.

## 5. Competitive positioning

- **Free or cheap tools exist:** Comprent (free), TenancyVault (free for one property), Landlord Alliance (about £10 a month), ComplyGuard (about £19 a month) and others from £7.99 a month.
- **Our angle:** a one-time purchase that the landlord owns and can adapt, with each rule linked to its official source, plus repairs and documents in the same place.
- **Honest risk:** free tools make willingness to pay the key unknown. Validate before building beyond v0.5.

## 6. Product specifications

- **Properties:** address, type, licence needs, owner, linked items.
- **Rule Library (reusable component):** item type, cycle in months, who it applies to, official source URL, last verified date.
- **Compliance Items:** property, rule, last done, next due (formula), days to due, status (Overdue / Due in 30 days / OK), certificate file.
- **Tenancies and Deposits:** tenant, start date, rent, deposit scheme, date protected, information served.
- **Repairs and Hazards:** reported date, category, response due, status, evidence.
- **Views:** compliance calendar, due in the next 60 days, by property, open repairs.

## 7. Feature list (planned)

- Rule-driven renewal dates and status flags
- Property compliance score (items OK ÷ total)
- Repairs log with response deadlines
- Deposit and tenancy checklist
- Renters' Rights Act transition checklist (bundles opportunity #10)
- Document vault per property
- Start Here, rule sources page and QA checklist
- v1.1 add-on: Making Tax Digital records workbook for landlords (opportunity #15)

## 1. Product name

**SOP Library System** (product) + **Done-for-You SOP Sprint** (service).

## 2. Product description

A Notion system for writing, organising, assigning and reviewing standard operating procedures, with a master SOP template, owners, review dates, version history and staff sign-off. The service writes a business's first 10 SOPs inside the system.

## 3. Target customer

- **Primary:** owner-led businesses with 5–50 staff, hiring or onboarding, with no operations manager.
- **Secondary:** operations consultants and virtual assistants who systemise businesses for clients.

## 4. Problem solved

Process knowledge sits in the owner's head, so quality varies and onboarding is slow. Research found that Trainual is quote-only with a $1,000 implementation fee, that smaller teams look for cheaper options, and that SOP writing services and SOP templates already sell. Evidence and sources sit on the linked opportunity record.

## 5. Competitive positioning

- **Training platforms** (Trainual and similar): powerful but priced and sized for larger teams.
- **Template packs:** cheap but no method, ownership or review cycle.
- **Freelance SOP writers:** documents, but often no system to keep them current.
- **Our angle:** system + method + optional done-for-you, inside the Notion workspace the business already uses, for a one-time price.

## 6. Product specifications

- **SOP Library:** title, department, owner, status (Draft / In review / Approved / Retired), version, last reviewed, review cycle, next review (formula), risk level, tools used.
- **Roles:** role, team, SOPs they must know.
- **Sign-offs:** person, SOP, version read, date.
- **Change Log:** SOP, version, change summary, approver, date.
- **Templates:** master SOP, checklist SOP, how-to-write-an-SOP guide, owner interview script, process inventory worksheet.
