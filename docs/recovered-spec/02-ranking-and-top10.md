## Scoring model (out of 100)

| Criterion | Points | High marks mean |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Customer pain | 20 | Costly, frequent or urgent problem |
| Evidence of demand | 20 | Several independent sources: forums, reviews, official data |
| Willingness to pay | 15 | Paid alternatives with published prices |
| Competition and differentiation | 10 | A clear gap against existing options |
| Development feasibility | 10 | We can build and deliver it ourselves |
| Acquisition feasibility | 10 | Reachable buyers and channels |
| Recurring revenue | 10 | Subscription or retainer potential |
| Scalability | 5 | Sells without matching effort per sale |

All 100 opportunities are ranked 1–100 in Market Opportunities, with each score stored on the record.

## What the deep dives changed

All 10 leaders sit in areas that had a deep dive. The EU's amendment of the AI Act's AI-literacy duty (Digital Omnibus, in force mid-July 2026) lowered the AI governance pack to #26 and AI literacy training to #43. The recruitment, agency, AI, creator and freight dives found cheap or free alternatives, which kept those areas out of the top 10.

## Why these three were chosen

The top three are the highest-scoring opportunities we can build and deliver ourselves now (build feasibility 9/10 each). They share components (account register, compliance calendar, document library) and audiences (UK small businesses and landlords). #4 is deferred until the kit proves demand; #5 becomes an upsell of #1.total 5012
